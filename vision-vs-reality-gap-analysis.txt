2. POSITIONING — "Financial Life OS" vs "Expense Tracker"
================================================================================

  VISION:
    Dabbu = Money + Relationships + Life Goals + Future Planning
    NOT just: Expense Tracker, Budget Planner, Family Expense Manager

  CURRENT STATE:
    - The app CAN track expenses, budgets, goals, health score
    - The app has couples and families features
    - But the app PRESENTS as an expense tracking app first
    - Onboarding screen doesn't frame Dabbu as a "Life OS"
    - The value prop of "Spaces" isn't front-and-center

    - No "What is Dabbu?" explanation that differentiates from Mint, Splitwise, YNAB

  WHAT'S NEEDED:
    - Onboarding flow that introduces: You → Your Spaces → Your Life Goals
    - Tagline/hero text in-app: "Your Financial Life Operating System"
    - Quick-start wizard: "What matters to you?" (House, Baby, Wedding, Car, etc.)

3. LIFE-BASED FINANCE (Events as first-class citizens)
================================================================================

  VISION:
    Nobody lives inside categories. People live inside events.
    Buying a house, marriage, baby, car, education, vacation, retirement
    should be FIRST-CLASS CITIZENS.

  CURRENT STATE:
    ✅ Backend has LifeEventDetectionEngine (AI-based detection)
    ✅ Backend Prisma has `lifeEvent` model
    ✅ Backend exposes GET /ai/life-events
    ✅ Mobile has GET /ai/life-events in cache TTL
    ✅ LifeHub has planners for: House, Baby, Retirement, Investment
    ❌ Life events are NOT shown on the Home Screen
    ❌ Life events are NOT shown as a timeline/feed anywhere
    ❌ No dedicated "Life Events" tab or screen
    ❌ Mobile never actually calls GET /ai/life-events (dead endpoint on mobile)
    ❌ Marriage/Wedding lifecycle not treated as a financial event
    ❌ Car purchase not treated as a financial event
    ❌ Education planning not treated as a financial event
    ❌ Users cannot manually create/confirm life events
    ❌ Life events have no impact on goals, budgets, or recommendations

  GAP:
    The backend detects life events from transaction patterns, but the mobile
    app never shows them to the user. Life events are the CORE differentiator
    of the vision, yet they're completely invisible in the UI.

  WHAT'S NEEDED:
    - "Life Events" section on the Home Screen (show detected events)
    - Allow users to confirm/dismiss detected events
    - Allow manual creation of life events (I'm planning a wedding)
    - Connect life events to Spaces (e.g., "Baby" event creates a Baby Space)
    - Show financial impact of life events (estimated costs, timelines)
    - Life event timeline view

4. SPACE-BASED FINANCE (User → Spaces)
================================================================================

  VISION:
    Most apps: User → ...
    Dabbu: User → Spaces → (Personal, Couple, Family, Home Construction,
    Europe Trip, Jaywin Business). Every financial activity belongs somewhere.

  CURRENT STATE:
    ✅ Prisma schema has Space model + spaceId on Transaction/Budget/Goal
    ✅ Backend has full Spaces CRUD controller (/spaces, /spaces/:id, etc.)
    ✅ Mobile has SpacesDashboardScreen, SpaceDetailScreen, CreateSpaceScreen
    ✅ Mobile has spaceStore (Zustand) with fetchSpaces, createSpace, etc.
    ✅ SpaceDetailScreen shows overview, money, goals, tasks, timeline, AI tabs
    ❌ AddExpenseScreen does NOT allow selecting a Space
    ❌ TransactionsList does NOT filter by Space
    ❌ GoalsListScreen does NOT filter by Space
    ❌ BudgetScreens do NOT filter by Space
    ❌ Home Screen does NOT let you switch between Spaces
    ❌ QuickAddBar does NOT include Space selection
    ❌ No "Default Space" concept for personal transactions
    ❌ Space creation doesn't set up default budgets/goals
    ❌ No visual indicator of which Space is active

  GAP:
    The data model supports space-based finance, but the UI treats Spaces as
    a separate feature rather than the CORE ORGANIZING PRINCIPLE. You can't
    add a transaction "to" a space. Spaces are just a separate tab.

  WHAT'S NEEDED:
    - Space selector on Add Expense / Quick Add
    - Space filter on Transactions, Goals, Budgets lists
    - Active Space indicator in the header
    - Home Screen filtered by active Space
    - Space creation wizard: "What kind of space?" (Personal, Couple, Family,
      Trip, Project, etc.) with pre-configured budgets/goals
    - Auto-create Personal Space on signup
